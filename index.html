Your data stays on this device. Files are read right here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server or saved anywhere. Refreshing the page clears it.

1. Upload an export file

WHOOP (CSV) or Apple Health (export.zip or export.xml). You can pick more than one WHOOP file.

How do I get my export?

Apple Health (iPhone): open the Health app › tap your profile picture (top right) → scroll down › Export All Health Data › send export.zip to this computer.

WHOOP: WHOOP app › menu › App Settings › Data Export. WHOOP emails you a zip. Unzip it and choose physiological_cycles.csv (and sleeps.csv if you like).

2. Use demo data

Explore with Maya, 24 — 60 days of made-up, WHOOP-style data with clear patterns.
