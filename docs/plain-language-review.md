# Plain-language review, run through the Kiro CLI

**Agent:** `.kiro/agents/plain-language-reviewer.json`, a read-only custom agent. Its resources
are our `accessibility.md` and `safety.md` steering files plus `docs/app-copy.txt`, which holds
every sentence the app shows (generated by `tools/export-app-copy.js`).

**Command:**

```bash
node tools/export-app-copy.js
kiro-cli chat --no-interactive --agent plain-language-reviewer --trust-tools=fs_read \
  "Review docs/app-copy.txt now, following your instructions."
```

The agent can only read files. We decided which suggestions to apply.

## Round 1: 47 sentences, 4 flagged, all applied

| Flag | What we changed |
|---|---|
| "bpm" not explained on a patient screen | Patient sentences now say "beats per minute". The AI prompt asks for the same. |
| The dizzy-pattern sentence was 42 words (limit 40) | Shortened to 34 words. |
| WHOOP "coming soon" text used "developer API", "data shape", "plug-in step" | Rewritten in everyday words. |
| "compared to your own baseline" on a patient screen | Now "compared to your own usual". |

## Round 2: 10 flagged, which taught us two things

- **A real problem:** the "pacing day" message became one 72-word sentence when all four signals
  were off. Our demo never shows that case, so no one had read it. We split it into three short
  sentences.
- **A problem in our own tooling:** 3 flags were sentence fragments. Our export script cut
  sentences apart at bold or italic words. That was our script's fault, not the app's, so we
  fixed the script.

## Round 3: 46 sentences, 5 flagged

| Flag | Decision |
|---|---|
| "recovery" (a WHOOP term) never explained | **Applied:** now "recovery score", including "your WHOOP recovery score". |
| "— in plain language —" in the patient intro adds nothing | **Applied:** removed. |
| "Consider taking it easy today and pacing yourself" is a behavioral nudge | **Kept, as a human judgment call.** It's the gentle pacing suggestion our requirements ask for (Req 4). It isn't a treatment or medication, and the safety guard agrees. |
| A few multi-sentence blocks counted as "over 40 words" | **Kept.** The rule is per sentence, and each sentence is under 40 words. |

We stopped after round 3. The reviewer is an AI. It found real problems, including one no one on
our team had seen, but it also over-flagged. Using it well meant reading each suggestion and
deciding, not applying everything automatically.
